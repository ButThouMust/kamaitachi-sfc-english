- Upon Reaching an Ending
When playing the game again after reaching an ending, you can start reading from
one of the chapters you had read on the way. Pick "Read from Chapter" on the
below screen and press Up or Down on the d-pad to select the chapter to start
reading from. Of course, making different decisions from your previous time
reading may change how the story plays out, and what ending you will see.
Additionally, depending on what endings you find, more choices will be added,
and the story will expand that much more.

Finally, there are many different kinds of endings. Did the end credits roll in
the ending you reached? If not, you cannot say yet that you have solved the
mystery.
